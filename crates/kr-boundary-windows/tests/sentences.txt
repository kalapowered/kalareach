# The sentences of the crate's refusals, failures and lines of progress that state a fact about the machine, and the read behind each.
# One row per sentence: <identifier> | <fixed words> | <sentence, shortened> | <the read behind it (the function and what it returned), or the rule or flow it rests on>.
# The identifier stands in the source as `// text: <identifier>` on the line before the place that makes the text. The fixed words are a
# fragment of that place's own source text (tests/sentences.rs fails where the place's text does not hold them, so a row of another place
# fails), and they hold no ` | `. For a place that passes a text on, the fixed words are the call that passes it on.
# A line `listed | <form> | <file> | <count> | <reason>` counts the places of a form in a file that carry no identifier; the case fails where
# the count is not the source's, so a new text of a listed form cannot come in without a row. Writing the read of a row is the reviewer's
# work: the case checks that every place has a row its own words hold and every row a place, not that the read is really behind its sentence.
